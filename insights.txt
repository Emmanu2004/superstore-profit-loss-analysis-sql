Key Insights
Insight 1

High sales do not always result in high profit.

Insight 2

Heavy discounts significantly reduce profit margins.

Insight 3

Certain categories consistently generated losses.

Insight 4

Regional profitability varied considerably.

Insight 5

Data quality directly impacts business decisions.

Business Recommendations
Review discount strategies on low-margin products
Focus on profitability instead of sales volume alone
Monitor loss-making categories regularly
Improve data quality validation processes
Use automated reporting for faster decision-making
